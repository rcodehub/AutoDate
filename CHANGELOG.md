# Changelog

Newest first. The latest entries are copied into the Unraid template (shown in Community Applications) each time an image is published.

### 2026-11-06
- First release: AutoDate scans a folder on a schedule and reads the original dates of the files and folders stored inside every zip, rar, 7z and tar archive (including multi-part sets) without extracting anything.
- Archives table with the archive date, the oldest and newest content dates and how much older the contents are, with sorting, search and an "Outdated only" filter (contents older than N years, default 5).
- Rename archives with the year of their contents (`name (2016).zip`), including every volume of a multi-part set, or delete them to the Recycle Bin, a trash folder or permanently. Both always show a preview first.
- Status page with live progress, a threads board with a Cancel button per archive, a waiting list for archives that are still downloading, a live log, and a **Scan folder…** button for one-off scans. Settings, History, Stats and 11 themes (light and dark), login with lockout.
